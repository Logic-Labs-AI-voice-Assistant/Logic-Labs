1. Technical Demonstration Prompts - By Category
A. Account & Access Management (Test Policy + Exception)
Standard Prompts:

"How do I reset my password? I forgot it."
"What are the password requirements? How long and what characters?"
"Where is the self-service password reset portal?"
"MFA is mandatory? What app do we use?"
"I lost my phone with Microsoft Authenticator, how to reset MFA?"
"I am a new hire starting next Monday, when will I get my laptop? My manager hasn't submitted the form yet."
Edge / Exception Prompts (Must catch contractor rule):
7. "I am a contractor and I need to reset my password, can I use portal.company.com/password?"
8. "Can you reset my MFA remotely over Teams call? I lost my device but can't come to office."
9. "What happens if my manager submits the New Hire IT Request only 2 days before my start date?"

B. Hardware & Equipment
"What laptop do I get as a standard employee?"
"I'm a developer, can I get a MacBook Pro 16 inch? What approval do I need?"
"My laptop screen is broken, can I open it and fix it myself?"
"How long will it take to get a loaner laptop if my laptop has critical failure?"
"When do I need to return the loaner laptop?"
"I need a second monitor and a standing desk, what is the policy?"
C. Software & Applications
"Can I install software from the internet on my work laptop?"
"I need Figma but it's not in Company Portal, what should I do?"
"Is it allowed to install Chrome extensions or gaming software?"
"How much OneDrive storage do I have? What about SharePoint site creation?"
"What happens if IT finds unauthorized software on my device?"
D. Network & Connectivity
"Which Wi-Fi should I connect to in the office? Can guests use Corp-Secure?"
"Do I need VPN for Microsoft 365 and ServiceNow when working from home?"
"What VPN do we use for remote work? Do I keep it on all day?"
"Can I connect my personal iPhone to Corp-Secure network?"
"I want to sync company email to my personal phone, is it allowed?"
E. Security & Compliance
"I got a suspicious email with a link, what should I do?"
"I clicked a phishing link and entered my password by mistake, what's the next step?"
"Where can I store customer PII and financial records? Can I store it on my desktop or USB drive?"
"What is the lock screen policy? How do I lock my screen on Windows and Mac?"
"What are the data classification levels?"
F. Out-of-Scope & Escalation (Critical for Hallucination Test)
"Can you help me fix my personal phone and home printer?"
"My home Wi-Fi is slow, can IT troubleshoot my ISP?"
"I have a question about my payroll and benefits, who do I contact?"
"The light in my office is broken and chair is broken, can IT help?"
"My IT ticket is not resolved for 3 days, how do I escalate it?"
"Is there a company-wide outage? Where do I check?"
G. Complex Multi-Policy Prompts (For Advanced Agent Demo)
"I'm working remotely, my laptop broke, and I need to access internal drives. What are the steps for loaner + VPN?"
"I'm a contractor, my laptop broke, I need Adobe Photoshop not in Company Portal, and I lost my MFA device. Walk me through it."
"Compare BYOD-Secure vs Corp-Secure vs Guest networks - what can each access?"
"I'm leaving my desk for 2 minutes to get coffee, do I really need to lock my screen? It auto-locks after 10 mins anyway."
